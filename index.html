THE AUTHOR'S STORY

A life can begin again.

My name is Toru Onuki. I grew up in Japan as a second-generation Jehovah's Witness. Religious life shaped my childhood and gave me values I still respect, including sincerity and consideration for others. It also involved restrictions that I found difficult.

At sixty, I wrote my memoir to reflect on the choices I made, the mistakes for which I take responsibility, and the freedom to decide how to live from here. I do not write to condemn individual believers or families.

Your past efforts were not meaningless. You are allowed to live your own life.
